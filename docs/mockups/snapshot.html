/snapshot — the one-shot pipeline inspector

One photo, read end to end, with every stage's evidence kept. This page is a diagnostic interface for the recognition algorithm, not a wizard: the eight stages always exist, always carry a state, and hold their full evidence behind a collapsed body. Nothing is gated behind an opt-in checkbox — diagnostics are the point.

1empty, and the photo just chosenshipped

2picking a corner by handshipped

Four taps place the corners; after that each is a draggable handle. The magnifier follows the finger so a corner can be placed under it.

3a full readshipped

Three columns: the photo with its quad, the position read, and the square inspector. Click any square to open it; click it again to step through that square's most likely readings.

4occupancy-only fallbackshipped

When the node has no piece bank for this set, it can still say which squares are occupied — and the page says exactly how to unlock the rest.

Also on the page, not drawn above: a history list of previous reads (each restores its whole pipeline by assignment), a ground-truth form that posts your hand corrections as labels, and per-square correction state that survives a re-read. The action bar sticks to the bottom of the viewport on a phone.
Note against the brief: /snapshot has no separate “upload” step. One file input labelled Take / choose photo (no capture attribute, so the native sheet offers camera and camera roll), and detection, rectification and recognition then run automatically — corner picking is a correction affordance, not a required step.
