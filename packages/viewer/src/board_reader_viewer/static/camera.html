calibrate

Calibrating adds to the piece bank rather than replacing it, so a second and a third angle are both worth capturing — a bank built from several viewpoints keeps reading the board when the camera moves or the light changes, which one frame's does not. The board must show the starting position, at rest, each time.

How framing works

Drag the four handles onto the playing surface's corners. a8 is wherever you call a8 — geometry cannot tell which corner is a8, so use rotate labels if they sit wrong.

The dashed box is what gets sent: it keeps headroom above the board so tall pieces are not decapitated at a real camera angle.

Once set, the corners follow the board — move the phone and they move with it, labels and all. If the page loses the board it freezes the quad where it last fitted rather than sending a wrong crop, and after a while looks for the board again by itself.

You rarely need any of this: the page looks for the board on its own as soon as the picture settles, and detect board asks it to look again.
